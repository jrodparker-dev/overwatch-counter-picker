This is a small project I'm putting together to get practice with Python files and inner workings. It is a simple app for now that takes the input of your role and an enemy hero
that you would like to counter and gives you the top 3 counter picks for your role that you can switch to. I plan to add a tkinter interface with additional options such as
team counter (tallies the counter score of the entire enemy team), and hero suggester (looks at your teammates and recommends you a hero that will fit that comp).
